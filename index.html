Introduction

Geophysical methods allow scientists to investigate the subsurface structure of the Earth using measurements taken at the surface.


In gravity surveys, variations in the Earth's gravitational field are caused by density contrasts between different rock bodies. Dense rocks produce positive gravity anomalies, while low-density rocks produce negative anomalies.


This project demonstrates a full workflow of gravity modeling and inversion using a synthetic geological model.

Simulation Workflow

The system simulates a complete geophysical interpretation pipeline:

  1. Model Construction
    Build a synthetic subsurface model with configurable density blocks.
  2. Forward Modeling
    Compute gravity anomalies caused by subsurface density variations.
  3. Noise Simulation
    Add realistic noise to mimic field survey data.
  4. Spectral Analysis
    Apply Fourier transforms to analyze frequency components of gravity data.
  5. Inverse Modeling
    Recover subsurface density distribution using regularized inversion.

About This Tool

GeoModeler Pro is an interactive geophysical modeling environment designed for educational and research purposes.


It integrates forward gravity modeling, spectral filtering, and inversion algorithms with interactive visualization to demonstrate how subsurface density structures influence observed gravity data.
